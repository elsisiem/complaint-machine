How It Works

What is this?

An AI agent that generates complaint letters and helps you send them through multiple channels - email, social media, and public complaint websites.

Novita AI (The Brain)

Generates the complaint letters, finds company contacts, and creates escalation content. Uses Llama 3.3 70B model.

ActionLayer (The Hands)

Executes real actions - sends emails, posts tweets, files BBB complaints. Connects your accounts to automate the process.

Escalation Ladder

1. Email CS - Direct to customer service

2. Email Exec - Bypass to executives

3. Tweet - Public shaming

4. BBB - Better Business Bureau

5. Ripoff Report - Public complaint database

6. PissedConsumer - Public reviews

7. Twitter Blast - Mass tweet at everyone
